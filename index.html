STUDENT ENGINEER / SAN JOSE, CA

Hi—I’m Pranav, a 10th grader at Evergreen Valley High School in San Jose. I like building things that move, sense, and respond to the real world.

I’ve spent four years in FIRST robotics across FLL, FTC, and FRC. I currently do mechanical work on FRC 2854 and design work on FTC 23425. Outside team seasons, I build robot arms, motion rigs, force-feedback hardware, electronics, and physical-AI projects.

I especially like projects where software has to control real hardware. A simulation can look perfect, but real motors, belts, sensors, wiring, and friction make the project harder—and more interesting.
