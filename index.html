PE STUDENT ENGINEER / SAN JOSE, CA

Hi—I’m Pranav, a 10th grader at Evergreen Valley High School in San Jose. I like building things that move, sense, and respond to the real world.

I’ve spent four years in FIRST robotics across FLL, FTC, and FRC. I currently do mechanical work on FRC 2854 and design engineering on FTC 23425. Outside team seasons, I build robot arms, motion rigs, force-feedback hardware, embedded devices, and physical-AI experiments.

I especially like projects where software has to survive contact with real hardware. A simulation can look perfect; motors, belts, sensors, friction, wiring, and manufacturing tolerances make the real system harder—and more interesting.

Current focus

Independent research · 2026–present

CATCH collision-safe robot joint

A low-cost, 3D-printed joint designed to stay rigid during normal operation and passively switch into a softer, still-controllable state during a hard collision. The design uses a cam, bearing roller, constant-force spring, rigid detent region, and catch ramp. It is currently in CAD, prototyping, mathematical modeling, and test planning.
