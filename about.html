About Pranav

I’m a high-school robotics builder from San Jose working across mechanical design, CAD, electronics, controls, and software to turn ideas into physical systems that can be built, tested, and improved.

01 / Story

I am most interested in robotics, mechanical engineering, mechatronics, physical AI, and mechanism design. I enjoy taking a project from an early idea to CAD, then turning it into real hardware through 3D printing, fabrication, wiring, programming, and testing.

Four years of FIRST robotics across FLL, FTC, and FRC taught me how to design around real constraints, work with a team, troubleshoot failures, and improve systems through repeated testing. I also compete in hackathons and build independent robot arms, force-feedback systems, motion simulators, embedded electronics, computer-vision systems, and AI projects.

I prefer systems that can be measured and tested over projects that stop at a concept or presentation. The best part is finding why a mechanism failed, changing it, and testing it again.

02 / Process

How I work

ENGINEERING DESIGN
LOOP MEASURE · IMPROVE
  1. 01 Research Study similar systems and past solutions.
  2. 02 Constraints Identify the most important mechanical and technical limits.
  3. 03 Design Sketch mechanisms and create the design in CAD.
  4. 04 Build Prototype and integrate motors, sensors, electronics, and software.
  5. 05 Test Use realistic conditions to find the failure points.
  6. 06 Iterate Modify the design, measure the result, and test again.
