About Pranav

I’m a high-school robotics builder from San Jose. I use CAD, electronics, controls, and software to turn ideas into real machines I can build, test, and improve.

01 / Story

I am most interested in robotics, mechanical engineering, and mechatronics. I like starting with an idea, drawing it in CAD, and then turning it into real hardware through 3D printing, fabrication, wiring, programming, and testing.

Four years of FIRST robotics across FLL, FTC, and FRC taught me how to work with a team, build around real limits, and fix things when they fail. I also compete in hackathons and build my own robot arms, force-feedback systems, motion simulators, electronics, computer-vision tools, and AI projects.

I like projects that can be measured and tested. My favorite part is finding out why something failed, changing it, and trying again.

02 / Process

How I work

ENGINEERING DESIGN
LOOP MEASURE · IMPROVE
  1. 01 Research Study similar systems and past solutions.
  2. 02 Constraints Identify the most important mechanical and technical limits.
  3. 03 Design Sketch mechanisms and create the design in CAD.
  4. 04 Build Prototype and integrate motors, sensors, electronics, and software.
  5. 05 Test Use realistic conditions to find the failure points.
  6. 06 Iterate Modify the design, measure the result, and test again.
